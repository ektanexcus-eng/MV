THE GAP WE WANT TO BRIDGE

The problem is not that India has no opportunities.
It is that connections are missing.

Every day, a young person searches for a job. A worker seeks better income. A woman wants financial independence. A small business needs the right person. A family needs reliable services. A senior citizen needs someone they can trust.

Somewhere, an opportunity may exist—but the person who needs it may not be able to find it. This is the gap Ekta NexCus wants to help bridge.

FROM SOCIAL NEED TO SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS

Social impact.
Sustainable operations.

Suvidha is our core platform concept: a simple gateway connecting people with opportunities and essential services. Technology should not replace people—it should help people connect better.

Our principle

People and organisations who can pay for value can help sustain an ecosystem that keeps essential access more affordable for people with fewer resources. Any subsidised access will depend on partnerships, operating capacity and a transparent model.

01

Employment & recruitment

Job postings, candidate sourcing, recruitment and workforce-support services for employers.

02

Local services marketplace

Connections between customers and service providers through suitable platform fees or commissions.

03

Healthcare connections

Discover consultations, home visits, diagnostics and other services through qualified providers.

04

Business & employer solutions

Local talent discovery, training support and community-focused workforce programmes.

05

Partnerships & CSR

Collaborate with companies and institutions on defined programmes, sponsorships and measurable outcomes.

06

Community & digital services

Selected memberships and digital services, while working to keep essential information accessible.

These are proposed revenue pathways, not claims of existing services or current revenue. Healthcare services will be provided by appropriately qualified and authorised partners.

Why this can become a business

Each community need can lead to a useful service. Each well-delivered service can create value and economic activity. Connecting those services responsibly can support both social outcomes and a sustainable operating model.

THE FUTURE OF WORK

आनंदी कामगार – प्रगतिशील भारत

Happy Workers – Progressive India. We are developing a research conversation around a six-hour workday concept: measuring outcomes and productivity, while exploring whether better-designed work can support family time, health, learning and wider employment. This is a proposal for research and pilots—not an existing policy or proven result.

“How many hours did you work?”
And also: what did you achieve, and what kind of life did your work allow you to live?
