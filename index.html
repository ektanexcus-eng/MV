01 — WHO WE ARE

A platform where
everyone has a role.

Ekta NexCus is a collaborative initiative focused on human wellbeing and the relationship between people, work and society.

We bring workers, farmers, young people, mentors, entrepreneurs, industries, researchers and community organisations into one ecosystem—so that needs can be understood, connections can be made and practical ideas can be tested.
